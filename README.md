
✦ Dress-Up Darling — Pixel Edition ✦
A kawaii dress-up game wrapped in a Y2K dream.
Dress-Up Darling is a browser-based fashion game with the cozy charm of early 2000s flash games and the aesthetic of a Windows XP desktop covered in stickers. Boot it up, and you're greeted by an animated title screen straight out of a visual novel — pastel pink blobs, floating hearts, and a chunky pixel logo.
Once you hit Start, a whole little desktop opens up. Drag the windows around, minimize them, click through the taskbar — it actually feels like you're using a tiny kawaii computer. The Mirror window shows your character standing on a pixel stage, and the Wardrobe window is your closet, packed with hand-drawn pixel clothing waiting to be tried on.
Click a top, a bottom, some shoes, a hairstyle — watch the layers stack up in real time. Every piece was drawn to fit her exactly, so nothing ever looks off. When you're happy with the look, hit Finish and head to the Final Look screen, where you can stamp your outfit with a pose — Slay, Cute, Dreamy, Iconic, or Darling.
It's small, it's sweet, and it runs entirely in your browser with no installs, no accounts, and no loading screens.

✦ Features

Animated Ren'Py-style title screen with pastel blob art
Draggable multi-window desktop UI with taskbar and clock
Hand-drawn pixel character with real-time layered dressing
Wardrobe with Hair, Tops, Bottoms, Dresses & Shoes
Final look screen with pose selector
Fully self-contained — one HTML file, works offline

Made for: fans of Y2K aesthetics, pixel art, and fashion games like Stardoll or early DeviantArt dress-up flash games.
